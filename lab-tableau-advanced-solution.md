# Tableau Lab – Advanced Visualizations
**By: Paulina Mamiaga**

## 📊 Overview
This project explores advanced Tableau visualization techniques to analyze customer behavior through:

- Choropleth Map (geographical distribution)
- Regression Plot (Income vs Customer Lifetime Value)
- Boxplot (Total Claim Amount by Vehicle Size)
- Interactive Dashboard
- Story summarizing analytical insights

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Geographic distribution:**  
  Customer concentration is highest in California, with surrounding states showing moderate presence, indicating a geographically concentrated customer base.

- **Income–CLV relationship:**  
  Income does not show a strong relationship with Customer Lifetime Value, as high-value customers are distributed across all income levels without a clear trend.

- **Vehicle Size analysis:**  
  Claim amounts are relatively similar across vehicle sizes, although midsize vehicles exhibit higher variability and more extreme outliers, suggesting slightly higher risk exposure.

- **Integrated dashboard findings:**  
  Customer value and risk are not driven by income or vehicle size, indicating that business performance depends more on behavioral patterns than on demographic or product characteristics.
